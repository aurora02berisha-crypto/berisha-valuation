What drives this estimate
This estimate is generated by an XGBoost gradient boosting model trained on approximately 45,000 Swiss residential property listings (2020–2023) and validated on 14,640 out-of-sample listings from 2024. The model achieved a median absolute percentage error (MdAPE) of 12.1% and R²=0.852 on the temporal test set. It represents a data-driven approximation and does not constitute a professional appraisal. Micro-location factors, interior quality, and market timing are not fully captured.
